At The Coffee Bar Point, we believe every cup of coffee tells a story. Since 2015, we have been serving freshly brewed coffee, delicious breakfasts, desserts, and healthy meals prepared with care and premium quality ingredients.

Over the years, we have continuously evolved to meet changing customer needs while maintaining our commitment to quality, hygiene, and affordability. Today, The Coffee Bar Point is more than just a café — it's a destination where great coffee, healthy food, and memorable moments come together.

WHAT IS A COLD BREW

The Coffee Point Coffee uses cold brew to make all its coffees, unlike most other coffees that are made using espressos. Cold brew is made by steeping coarsely ground coffee in cold water for 16-36 hours, resulting in a coffee which is smoother, less acidic and has higher caffeine content.
